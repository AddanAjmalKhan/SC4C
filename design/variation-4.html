How much is my scrap car worth?

It depends on the vehicle’s weight, make, model, condition and current metal prices. Share your car’s details for a personal offer. The quote is free, and you decide whether to accept.

Is pickup really free?

Yes. Towing is included for scrap car pickups within our GTA service area. Confirm your location and access details with the team when arranging collection.

Will you buy a car that doesn’t run?

Yes. We accept vehicles with mechanical issues and accident damage. Let us know about missing parts or access restrictions so we can give you an informed quote and arrange the right pickup.

What should I have ready for pickup?

Have your vehicle ownership document and matching government-issued photo ID ready. Remove your personal belongings. If your ownership document is missing, call us before arranging pickup.

Can you pick up my car today?

Same-day pickup may be available, depending on your location and our schedule. Call 437-269-6321 to check the next available slot, including weekends.

When do I get paid?

You receive the agreed payment when we collect the vehicle. Confirm your car’s details and the offer with our team before booking your pickup.
