FAQ

Still Need Help?

Call Us: +1 437-269-6321
How much will I get for my scrap car?

Between $250 and $2000+ depending on the vehicle’s make, model, weight, and condition. Call for a free quote.

Do you pick up non-running cars?

Yes! We tow all vehicles regardless of condition, running or not.

Is the towing really free?

100% free pickup with zero hidden charges, anywhere in the GTA.

How long does the scrap car removal process take?

Most pickups happen the same day you call, often within a few hours, at a time that works for your schedule.

Do I need the ownership papers to sell my scrap car?

Yes, bring your vehicle ownership and a valid ID. We handle the ownership transfer and deregistration on the spot.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Toronto and the entire GTA, including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Scarborough, North York, Etobicoke, Oakville, and Burlington.

What documents do I need to have ready?

Bring your vehicle ownership (registration) and a government-issued photo ID that matches the name on the ownership. If you’ve lost your ownership, tell us when you call, we can usually still process the sale.

Do you buy cars without a working engine or transmission?

Yes. Missing an engine, transmission, or catalytic converter lowers the offer, but it does not stop us from buying the vehicle.

How do you calculate my cash offer?

We price your vehicle using its weight, make and model, condition, and current scrap metal prices. We explain the number before you agree to anything, so there are no surprises at pickup.

Can I get a quote without committing to sell?

Yes. Every quote is free and no-obligation. You decide whether to move forward once you hear the offer.
