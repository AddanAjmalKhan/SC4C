Pricing

What Determines Your Quote

Five factors set your cash offer. We walk you through each one before you accept.

SC4C · Spec Sheet
FactorHow It Affects Your Offer
01Vehicle WeightHeavier vehicles, such as trucks and SUVs, contain more recyclable steel and typically earn a higher scrap value.
02Make & ModelVehicles with in-demand parts, such as popular engines or transmissions, often fetch a higher offer than the metal value alone.
03ConditionA complete, intact vehicle is worth more than one missing an engine, catalytic converter, or major parts.
04Current Scrap Metal PricesSteel and metal commodity prices shift weekly and directly move our cash offers up or down.
05Pickup LocationVehicles in easily accessible locations within the GTA core cost less to tow, which can improve your offer.

Based on these factors, most vehicles we pick up in the GTA sell for $250 to $2000, with larger trucks and newer write-offs going higher.
